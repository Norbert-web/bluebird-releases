# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the Bluebird OS project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being kind and respectful in all interactions
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them get started
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Acknowledging and crediting others' contributions

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention
- Sustained disruption of community discussions
- Discrimination based on any personal characteristic

---

## Enforcement

Community leaders (the LAMN-NOBERT team) are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They will take fair corrective action in response to any behavior that is harmful, threatening, or inappropriate.

**Consequences for violations:**
1. **Warning** — A private written warning for minor first offenses
2. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from community spaces for repeated or serious offenses
3. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal for severe violations or sustained unacceptable behavior

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:
- Opening a confidential GitHub issue marked **[CONDUCT]**
- Messaging the LAMN-NOBERT team directly on Telegram or Discord

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Bluebird community spaces — GitHub, Telegram, Discord, Reddit, YouTube comments, and any other official channel — and also when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
